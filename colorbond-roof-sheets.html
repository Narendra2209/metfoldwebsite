Profiles

Find the right sheet for the roof.

Corrugated

The classic Aussie roof. Lightweight, cost-effective and tough, with a timeless wave for homes, sheds and commercial builds.

5-Rib sheet

Bold, linear ribs for a sharper modern look — at home on roofs, wall cladding, decorative facades and fencing.

Cliplock 700

A high-strength, concealed-fix profile for flat and low-pitch roofs down to 2°. Long lengths mean fewer end laps and clean, flat pans.

Metrib

A versatile rib profile for roofs and walls, straight or curved, inside or out — in COLORBOND® and ZINCALUME® finishes.

Mini Corodek

Corrugated character with smaller, lower ribs for a smooth, refined finish. Use it inside or out.

Wallclad panel

A clean, modern steel wall panel with excellent weather resistance for homes and commercial buildings.

### Typography
```css
Font Family: 'Inter', sans-serif
Hero: 64px → 28px (responsive)
Section Headings: 42px → 28px
Card Titles: 20-32px
Body: 16-20px

```

### Spacing
```css
Section Padding: 100px → 60px
Grid Gaps: 32px → 24px → 16px
Card Padding: 48px → 32px → 24px
Consistent margins throughout
```


## 🎭 Animations & Effects

### Entrance Animations
- **fadeIn**: General content (0.8s)
- **fadeInUp**: Hero titles, cards (0.8-1s)
- **fadeInDown**: Subtitles (0.6s)
- **fadeInRight**: Sidebar (0.8s)

### Hover Effects
- **Cards**: Lift 8-12px + shadow increase
- **Icons**: Scale 1.1-1.15 + rotate 360°
- **Images**: Scale 1.03-1.08
- **Buttons**: Gradient slide + lift 2px
- **Borders**: Color transition + scale
- **Lists**: Slide right 4-8px

### Timing
- **Fast**: 0.2-0.3s (buttons, borders)
- **Standard**: 0.3-0.4s (cards, hover)
- **Slow**: 0.5-0.8s (images, entrance)

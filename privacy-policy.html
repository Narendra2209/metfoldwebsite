Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Last updated October 2026

Metfold Sheetmetal (“Metfold”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our branches, how we use it and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Who we are

Metfold Sheetmetal supplies roofing, rainwater goods, cladding and custom fabrication from branches in Sunbury, Melton and Pakenham, Victoria. Our website address is metfoldsm.com.au.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, company, phone number, email and delivery address when you call, email, visit a branch, send an order form or use an enquiry form.
  • Order and account information such as products, quantities, colours, site details and, for trade accounts, the details provided on our credit application.
  • Website information such as basic technical data your browser sends (for example device type and pages visited) when you browse the site.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, process and deliver orders, manage trade accounts, provide customer support and improve our products and website. We don’t sell your personal information.

Forms, email and your quote list

Enquiry and quote forms on this website open your own email app with the details filled in — nothing is sent until you press send. Items you add to your quote list are stored only in your browser on your device, and you can clear them at any time from the quote list page.

Cookies and third-party content

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and 3D graphics code from a public content delivery network. These services may receive your IP address and browser information when you visit. Links to maps and downloadable PDFs open content hosted by those services or on our servers.

Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to run our business — for example with delivery providers, accounting and IT service providers, or where required by law. These parties are expected to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as it’s needed for the purposes above, including to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, and then securely delete or de-identify it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a complaint about how we’ve handled it. We’ll respond within a reasonable time. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email Orders@metfoldsm.com.au or call our Sunbury branch on (03) 9732 0148.

Sumps

Sumps to your exact specification.

Our team fabricates every kind of sump — standard, high-sided and step — to suit your box gutter and flow requirements. Sumps sit in the gutter sole and drop water into the downpipe or a rainwater harvesting system.

Rainheads and sumps are close cousins: the main difference is where they sit on the roof. Both can be fitted with overflows and leaf guards, and both stop leaves and twigs entering the plumbing.

  • Boxed, round and tapered rainheads
  • Heritage styles for classic homes
  • COLORBOND® steel or copper
